The injection of medication directly into the muscle.

Injection sites:
- [[Vastus Lateralis]]
- Deltoids
- Gluteus Maximus
- Gluteus Medius

In general the recommendation for self-injection is the [[Vastus Lateralis]], because of its accessibility and size.

For this site, the recommended needle size is: 1 inch 25 [[Needle Gauge|G]]. Smaller [[Needle Gauge|gauges]] (i.e. larger needles) are acceptable, but will likely hurt more. It is not recommended use anything less than 23 G. Large [[Needle Gauge|gauges]] are unlikely to be available for the length (because they are thinner, they're more fragile).
