An [[Anti-androgen]]. Reduces both production of [[Testosterone|T]], and sensitivity to [[Testosterone|T]]. It is a potassium-sparing [[diuretic]]. This means electrolytes are evacuated during urination, *except* potassium. This causes a tendency to accumulate potassium.

This is the only FDA-approved [[Anti-androgen|AA]], and also arguably one of the worst.

Don't eat bananas (or any other potassium-rich foods) while on Spiro. Unchecked potassium consumption can lead to [[Hyperkalemia]].

Monitor potassium and sodium.
