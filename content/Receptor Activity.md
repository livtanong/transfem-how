The degree to which a chemical (this includes but is not exclusive to [[Hormone]]s) activates a [[Receptor]]. Not to be confused with [[Receptor Affinity]].

Examples 
- Caffeine has high [[Receptor Affinity]] but effectively zero [[Receptor Activity]] for the Adenosine [[Receptor]] (which, when activated, makes us feel tired).
- [[Estradiol|E2]] and [[Estrone|E1]] can both activate the estrogen receptor. [[Estradiol|E2]] has a higher activity than [[Estrone|E1]], and thus has greater effect.
