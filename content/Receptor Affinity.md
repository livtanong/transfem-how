aliases:
  - Affinity
---
How readily a chemical (this includes but is not exclusive to [[Hormone]]s) binds to a [[Receptor]]. Not to be confused with [[Receptor Activity]]

Examples
- Caffeine has high [[Receptor Affinity]] but effectively zero [[Receptor Activity]] for the Adenosine [[Receptor]] (which, when activated, makes us feel tired).
- [[Estradiol|E2]] and [[Estrone|E1]] are both drawn to the estrogen receptor, but [[Estradiol|E2]] has greater affinity to the [[Estrogen Receptor]] than [[Estrone|E1]], which is why the presence of [[Estrone|E1]] is not so much a concern provided that the E2:E1 ratio is high.
