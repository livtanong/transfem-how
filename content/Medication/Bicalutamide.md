Greatly reduces sensitivity to [[Testosterone|T]]. Your body will produce lots of [[Testosterone|T]], but won't react to it. In rare cases, can cause liver toxicity and/or lung toxicity. But very rare. 

Bonus: To compensate for the reduced sensitivity, your body will produce more [[Testosterone|T]], and your [[Aromatase]] will give you extra [[Estradiol|E2]] for free.
