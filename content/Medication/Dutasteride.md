So far, the most potent [[5α-Reductase Inhibitor|5ar inhibitor]]. Like [[Finasteride]], originally developed for the treatment of [[Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia]].

Like [[Finasteride]], also used to [[Inhibitor|inhibit]] the conversion of [[Testosterone|T]] to [[Dihydrogen Testosterone|DHT]].
