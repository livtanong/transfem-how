Medication that aims to either prevent the production of [[Testosterone|T]], or reduce the receptor sensitivity of [[Testosterone|T]]. These medications usually either: have unwanted side-effects, or are prohibitively expensive. This is why the [[Estradiol Monotherapy|E2 Monotherapy]] is usually desired if possible.

Examples:
- [[Spironolactone]]
- [[Cyproterone Acetate]]
- [[Bicalutamide]]
- [[GnRH Agonist]]
