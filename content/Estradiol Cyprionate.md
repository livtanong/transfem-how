A relatively stable [[Ester]] of [[Estradiol|E2]] that has a [[Half-life|half-life]] of 8-10 days. (Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estradiol_cypionate) Because of this stability, weekly injections become viable without much fluctuation in [[Serum]] [[Estradiol|E2]] levels.

Considered functionally equivalent to [[Estradiol Enanthate]].
