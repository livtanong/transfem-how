The amount of time it takes for the quantity of a given thing (usually a chemical) to be halved. In the context of HRT, how long it takes for the body to eliminate half of the initial quantity of medication you take. The longer the half-life, the longer it stays in the body.

Examples:
- IM [[Estradiol Valerate|EV]]
