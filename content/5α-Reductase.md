The enzyme that convert [[Testosterone|T]] to [[Dihydrogen Testosterone|DHT]]. In some cases, converts [[Progesterone|P4]] to [[Dihydrogen Testosterone|DHT]]. This second pathway is known as the [[Androgen Backdoor Pathway]]. If you start masculinizing or losing hair while taking [[Progesterone|P4]], you may have this pathway, though this is not conclusive.
