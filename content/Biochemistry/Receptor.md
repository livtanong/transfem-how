They're like locks or gates that [[Receptor Binding|bind]] and [[Receptor Activity|activate]] when a chemical with a very specific shape comes into contact with it. 

Similarly shaped molecules can trigger the same receptors. But the effect they produce might be slightly different. For example, [[Estradiol|E2]] and [[Estrone|E1]] can both activate the estrogen receptor. [[Estradiol|E2]] has a greater effect than [[Estrone|E1]]. 

Likewise, [[Dihydrogen Testosterone|DHT]] and [[Testosterone|T]] activate the testosterone receptor. Fun fact, caffeine and theobromine (one of the molecules in chocolate) have similar shapes, and bind to the Adenosine Receptor.
