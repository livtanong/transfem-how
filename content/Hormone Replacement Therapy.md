Yes, I know, the more correct term is [[Hormone Replacement Therapy|Gender Affirming Hormone Therapy]]. But "HRT" is the more popular term, and also includes hormone therapy that isn't necessarily for the purpose of being gender affirming. e.g. HRT for menopausal women.

The introduction of [[Exogenous Hormones]] into the body to compensate for the lack of desired hormones.
